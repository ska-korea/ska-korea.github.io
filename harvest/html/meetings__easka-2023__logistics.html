Travel guide



Connection between Airport and Venue


There is an Airport Limousine Bus No. 600 (KRW 4,500 one way), in front of Gate 5 of the airport, directly connecting the Jeju International Airport and the Suites Hotel. It takes ~50 minutes, and the time gap between buses is 15 ~ 30 minutes.


  • Jeju International Airport -> Suites Hotel bus stop

    • The first bus: 06:00

    • The last bus: 22:50


  • Suites Hotel bus stop -> Jeju International Airport

    • The first bus: 06:40

    • The last bus: 22:20

    • Please carefully check with the driver that the bus is going to the airport, NOT to Seogwipo.
