2024 SKA-Korea Winter Workshop

Dec. 18th-19th, 2024

KW Convention, Daejeon, Korea


Rationale


In preparation for the SKA era, the SKA-Korea Working Group is hosting a winter workshop to assess the current status of the SKA project and discuss scientific and technological research topics related to SKA. 

This workshop will cover presentations on SKA scientific and technological research topics, review the status of data centers, discuss the progress of SKA Data Challenge (SDC), and explore Korea's participation strategy in the SKA project. 

Topics

  • SKA Scientific and Technological Research

  • Status Report on SKA Regional Centre (SRC)

  • SKA Data Challenge (SDC) 3b Activities

  • Report on International Research Visits to SKA Institutions

  • Discussion of SKA-Korea 2025
