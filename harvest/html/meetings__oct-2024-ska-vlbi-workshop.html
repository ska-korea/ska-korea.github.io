Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute (KASI), Daejeon, Korea

Ramada Deajeon Hotel, Daejeon, Korea


Rationale


As the Square Kilometre Array (SKA) project moves into its deployment phase, it is a timely moment to revisit discussions on SKA-VLBI (Very Long Baseline Interferometry). VLBI is a field where the Korean radio astronomy community possesses expertise, and with Korea's participation in the SKA project from 2025, there is a growing interest in SKA-VLBI within the Korean SKA group.


In light of these developments, we are organizing a workshop designed to

- assess the current status of SKA-VLBI,

- evaluate future directions for SKA-VLBI, considering the latest scientific and technological advancements,

- explore potential roles and contributions of the Korean group in SKA-VLBI initiatives.


We aim to bring together experts in SKA-VLBI, including key figures from the Jumping JIVE "VLBI with SKA" project and the SKA-VLBI Working Group. The workshop will feature a series of seminars along with contributed talks, complemented by pre and post discussion sessions.


We encourage all those interested in SKA-VLBI to participate in this workshop.

Topics


  • SKA-VLBI science review

  • SKA-VLBI operation

  • SKA and SKA-VLBI commensality

  • Multi-view technique

  • Correlation and user support
