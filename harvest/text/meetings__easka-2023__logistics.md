## Travel guide

### Connection between Airport and Venue

There is an Airport Limousine Bus No. 600 (KRW 4,500 one way), in front of Gate 5 of the airport, directly connecting the Jeju International Airport and the Suites Hotel. It takes ~50 minutes, and the time gap between buses is 15 ~ 30 minutes.

                  - Jeju International Airport -> Suites Hotel bus stopThe first bus: 06:00The last bus: 22:50

                  - Suites Hotel bus stop -> Jeju International AirportThe first bus: 06:40The last bus: 22:20Please carefully check with the driver that the bus is going to the airport, NOT to Seogwipo.
