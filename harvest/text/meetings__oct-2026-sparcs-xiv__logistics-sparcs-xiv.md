## From Incheon International Airport to Building D

By train (recommended)

                  1. After customs and immigration, follow the signs for the Airport Railroad (AREX).
                  2. Buy a T-money card and load it with money (see Travel tips below).
                  3. Take the All-stop train to Magongnaru Station (마곡나루역). Do not take the Express train: it doesn’t stop at Magongnaru and runs straight to Seoul Station.
                  4. Leave by Exit 6. Photo directions to Building D are below.

The trip takes about 40 min from Terminal 1 or 48 min from Terminal 2 and costs about KRW 4,350.

By limousine bus

Limousine buses are more convenient if you have large suitcases. Bus 6003 goes to Magok Station (마곡역), about a 20-minute walk from the venue. The fare is about KRW 17,000, and the trip takes 40–60 min.

By taxi

Taxis wait outside the arrivals hall at both Terminal 1 and Terminal 2. The ride takes about 45 min and costs about KRW 40,000 (~USD 30).

                  - Ignore touts in the arrivals hall. Go to the official taxi stand, where drivers use the meter.
                  - Have your hotel address written in Korean to show the driver.
                  - Foreign credit cards generally work in taxis, but carry some cash just in case.
                  - Uber works in Korea, but it only books local taxis.

## Travel tips

### T-money card

                  - T-money is the transport card used on subways, buses, and taxis throughout Korea.
                  - The card costs KRW 5,000. You can buy it at convenience stores (GS25, CU, Emart24) or from vending machines at the airport.
                  - Topping up is mostly cash only. Some newer subway vending machines now accept overseas cards for top-ups.
                  - You cannot tap a foreign credit card or phone to ride the subway or buses. You need a T-money card.

![](images/meetings__oct-2026-sparcs-xiv__logistics-sparcs-xiv_71)

Maps
 Google Maps works poorly in Korea. We recommend Kakao Map or Naver Map, which both have English versions.

SIM cards
 SIM and eSIM cards are sold in the arrivals hall. Dosirak SIM is generally a good option.

Learn to read Korean!
 The Korean alphabet (Hangul) takes about 15 minutes to learn, and it makes signs and maps much easier to read:[https://www.ryanestrada.com/learntoreadkoreanin15minutes/](https://www.ryanestrada.com/learntoreadkoreanin15minutes/)

### Money

                  - The currency is the South Korean Won (KRW). Prices are often round numbers in thousands (e.g., 1,000 or 10,000 KRW).
                  - KRW 1,000 ≈ USD 0.75 ≈ AUD 1.07 ≈ EUR 0.66
                  - Cards are widely accepted, but keep some cash for T-money top-ups and small shops.
                  - ATMs: look for a Global ATM. Not every foreign card works at every Global ATM. e.g., KB Global ATMs.

### Evenings out

                  - Hongdae (홍대, Hongik University area): the closest nightlife area to the venue. It is 2 stops (~11 min, KRW 1,650) from Magongnaru on the Airport Railroad and is packed with bars, cafés, and live music.

Other areas worth visiting

                  - Itaewon: a famous international district near the former US base.
Itaewon Station (이태원역), Line 6, Exit 1
                  - Jongno 3-ga: a mix of “old-school” Korea and newer spots, popular with all ages, within walking distance of Insadong.
 Jongno 3-ga Station (종로3가역), Lines 1/3/5, Exit 4
                  - Euljiro: nicknamed “Hipjiro” by locals, with hipster bars and cafés in old print-shop alleys.
 Euljiro 3-ga Station (을지로3가역), Lines 2/3, Exit 10
                  - Gangnam: a large, upscale district, further from the venue.
 Gangnam Station (강남역), Line 2, Exit 5
                  - Bongeunsa: a Buddhist temple near Gangnam, with the Gangnam Style statue close by.
 Bongeunsa Station (봉은사역), Line 9, Exit 1 (temple) / Exit 7 (statue)
                  - Note that the excursion area is close to Jongno 3-ga and Euljiro
