## Venue

2026 SPARCS XIV will be held at SEOUL MICE PLAZA in Seoul.

Address: 7F, Building D, Le West City, 143 Magokjungang-ro, Gangseo-gu, Seoul, Korea

Getting to the Venue:

The venue is on the 7th floor, accessible via the elevators, which you will see as you enter the Building D entrance.

Upon arriving on the 7th floor, directional signage will be available to guide you to the plaza.
