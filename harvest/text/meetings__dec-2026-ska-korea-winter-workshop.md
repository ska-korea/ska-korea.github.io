# Dec 2026 SKA Korea Winter Workshop

<!-- 원본: https://sites.google.com/view/ska-korea/meetings/dec-2026-ska-korea-winter-workshop -->

2026년 SKA Korea 겨울 워크숍이 12월 16일(수)부터 18일(금)까지 연세대학교 캠퍼스에서 개최됩니다. 많은 분들의 관심과 참여 바랍니다.

The 2026 SKA Korea Winter Workshop will be held on the Yonsei University campus from Wednesday, December 16, to Friday, December 18. We look forward to your interest and participation.

Date & Time: December 16 Wednesday 1PM - 18 Friday 1PM

Venue: 연세대학교, 백양누리 B146, 곽정환 홀 / B146 Baekyangnuri, Kwak Joung-Hwan Challenge Hall, Yonsei University, Seoul

The Square Kilometre Array (SKA) will be the world's largest radio telescope. It is now being built and getting closer to its first science phases, while its precursor and pathfinder telescopes (MeerKAT, ASKAP, MWA) are already producing exciting new results. Korea is now formally joining the SKA Observatory, and the role of the Korean community keeps growing.

As Korea's part in the SKA becomes more active, the SKA Korea Winter Workshop 2026 aims to share the SKA science being done and planned in Korea, and to look for ways to connect with large international projects based on SKA precursors and pathfinders, including chances for postdocs and students to take part. Building on this, the workshop also aims to strengthen teamwork among Korean SKA groups and to discuss clear ways for students and early-career researchers to join international collaborations.

We especially encourage students in the SKA Korea funding programmes to present. Talks on new ideas, ongoing work, or early results are all welcome.

Registration: Closed

Program Outline:

Main theme - "Towards SKA1 - Strengthening Korean participation and international collaboration": The workshop aims to share SKA-related scientific and technological developments, strengthen national and international collaboration, and explore future opportunities for the Korean SKA community, particularly students and early-career researchers.

● SKA-Korea: Current status and future opportunities

○ Korea's participation in SKAO, and future plans

● SKA science in Korea and international developments

○ Recent scientific results and ongoing research activities

○ Updates on precursor projects

● Student and postdoctoral research presentations

○ Ongoing projects, preliminary results, and new research ideas

● Instrumentation, technology, and industry engagement

○ Technology development in Korea and potential contributions to SKA1

○ Industrial participation and procurement opportunities

● Strategic discussions: Korea's pathway to SKA1

○ National and international science collaboration, including potential key science programmes

○ Technology development and SRC/computing infrastructure

○ Early-career development, training, and international opportunities
