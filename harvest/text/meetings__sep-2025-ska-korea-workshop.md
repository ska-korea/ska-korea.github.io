Program

                  - 12:30–13:00 Welcome!
                  - 13:00–13:10 (10 min) Opening Remarks and Introduction
                  - 13:10–13:35 (25 min) Xiaopeng Cheng (KASI), LeMMINGs and AGN surveys
                  - 13:35–13:55 (20 min) Min-Ji Kim (KASI/UST), SKA-Mid VLBI Simulation
                  - 13:55–14:15 (20 min) Sieun Go (KASI/UST), SKA-Low VLBI Simulation
                  - 14:15–14:35 (20 min) Minsu Kim (Sejong Univ./KASI), HI Super-Profile Analysis of AVID Galaxies

                  - 15:00–15:25 (25 min) Chandra Shekhar Saraf (KASI), Estimating n(z) for SKA Continuum Surveys with Cross-Correlations
                  - 15:25–15:45 (20 min) Min-Gyu Ryu (UNIST), Machine learning processing of the SKA Data Challenge 1

                  - 16:05–16:30 (25 min) Chan Park (Henan Academy of Science), International Research Trends in Pulsar Timing Arrays

                  - 17:00 Group Photo
                  - 18:00 Dinner
