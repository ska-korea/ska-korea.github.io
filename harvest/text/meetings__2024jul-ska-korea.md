### 7월 25일 (목) 13:00 - 18:30pm (18:30pm ~ 저녁 식사)

                  - 13:00 - 13:30: 등록

[Session 1] Chair: 손봉원 (한국천문연구원)

                  - 13:30 - 13:35: 류동수 (UNIST) 환영사
                  - 13:35 - 14:20 (I): 김재영 (경북대학교) Introduction to VLBI
                  - 14:20 - 14:35 (C): 박종호 (경희대학교) Faraday Rotation Observed in Active Galactic Nuclei: Applications in the SKA Era
                  - 14:35 - 14:50 (C): Jeff Hodgson (세종대학교) QUOKKAS in the SKA era
                  - 14:50 - 15:10: 휴식

[Session 2] Chair: 윤혜인 (서울대학교)

                  - 15:10 - 15:55 (I): 김준한 (KAIST) VLBI: Receiver System
                  - 15:55 - 16:40 (I): 염재환 (한국천문연구원) VLBI: Correlator
                  - 16:40 - 17:00: 휴식

[Session 3] Chair: 김준한 (KAIST)

                  - 17:00 - 17:15 (C): 이덕형 (경북대학교) EVN 5 GHz e-VLBI observations of SN 2023ixf in M101
                  - 17:15 - 18:00 (I): 안경진 (조선대학교) SKA science data challenge 3b (SDC3b)
                  - 18:00 - 18:15 (C): David Parkinson (한국천문연구원) Cross-correlations of the Evolutionary Map of the Universe with other extragalactic survey data

                  - 18:30 - : 저녁 만찬 (화샨샤브 [https://naver.me/Fu6KCue3](https://naver.me/Fu6KCue3))
