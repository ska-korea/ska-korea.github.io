# 2025 SKA-Korea Summer Workshop

Dates: June 25-26 (Wed-Thu)

Venue: Hotel Onoma, Daejeon (5th floor Grand Ballroom)

Registration

                  - Please fill out the following Google Form by June 6 (Fri): [https://forms.gle/siCkfxGwP22WRKkX6](https://forms.gle/siCkfxGwP22WRKkX6)
                  - No registration fee
                  - Dinner banquet (June 25) included with an opt-in
                  - Accommodation (June 25) will be provided on a first-come, first-served basis. A limited number of rooms with double occupancy are reserved at the nearby LOTTE City Hotel and will be preferably offered to students and post-docs.
